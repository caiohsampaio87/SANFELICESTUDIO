✱ THE ARTIST

Caio Sanfelice is a photographer and image-maker. An MA graduate of the London College of Communication, his work has been exhibited and published internationally, including Dazed and Vogue Italia, and is held in the institution's permanent collection

His practice spans photography, creative direction and set production. The disciplines are not treated as separate crafts: photography, fashion, painting and set design operate within a single practice, in which the artist is present at every stage — conception, construction and execution. The resulting image is not a record of a found scene; it is the final object of a constructed one. In 2026, Caio joins UAL's Creative Business Accelerator to develop Caio Sanfelice Studio as a multidisciplinary house for exhibition, publication and collaboration.

The result doesn't document a scene — it constructs one.
