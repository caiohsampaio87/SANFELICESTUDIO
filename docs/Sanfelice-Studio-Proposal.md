# Project Proposal — Sanfelice Studio Portfolio Website

A 3-page responsive portfolio website (HTML/CSS) for Sanfelice Studio, a London-based commercial photographer specialising in fashion, beauty, still life and portraits, who also produces sets and backgrounds as an artist. The site gives small fashion and cosmetics brands a curated, permanent showcase of his best work — currently buried in a fast-moving Instagram feed — and makes requesting a quote effortless.

## Pages

1. **Home** — full-image hero with tagline and a 6-image featured grid.
   *Purpose:* make an instant first impression on style and quality, and route visitors to the work within one click.
2. **Work (Services)** — gallery filtered by category (Fashion · Beauty · Still Life · Portraits), plus a Services section (including creative direction & set design) and a "How it works" strip (Brief → Quote → Shoot → Delivery).
   *Purpose:* let a brand owner find examples similar to their project and understand the full offer.
3. **About** — portrait and the artist's story: set design and production background, London base, links to Instagram and Substack.
   *Purpose:* build trust in his creative vision, not just his camera.

Every page carries a fixed contact bar (WhatsApp + email), so contact is always zero clicks away.

## Competitor Websites Researched

- https://www.alfonsoscarpa.com — London fashion/portrait/editorial photographer for models, artists and creative brands. Positioning very close to Sanfelice's; benchmark for combining commercial and artistic identity.
- https://www.davidesolla.com — London fashion & editorial photographer with beauty portraits, campaigns and lookbooks. Model for organising a gallery by category.
- https://www.lukechapmanphotography.co.uk/portrait-portfolio — editorial-style portraits with fashion-driven styling for creatives and professionals. Reference for a clean portrait gallery layout.

Unlike most competitors, Sanfelice also offers set design and creative direction in-house — the site's Services section makes this a differentiator.
